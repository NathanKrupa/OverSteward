Foundation pages vs the SERP leaders: gap analysis and the road over

What ProPublica, Instrumentl, Cause IQ, GrantStation and GrantMakers.io actually serve for "<foundation> grants" queries — verbatim, fetched live — and a phased plan to move our 3,439 enriched foundation pages into their vicinity.

Method: internal code + live-page audit of aigranthelper.com, direct fetches of competitor profile pages for the same foundations (EJAF, Moses Taylor, Dover), and a 104-agent deep-research pass with 3-vote adversarial verification on every claim. 11 findings survived; 1 was refuted and is excluded.

The verdict in three sentences. Our technical base is genuinely good — server-rendered pages, a principled noindex gate (3,439 indexable of ~136k shells), sitemaps, IndexNow, edge caching — but our metadata is prose-flavored where every winner is number-forward, our ~1,400 words trail the 2,500–4,000-word data-rich benchmark, and our FAQPage markup earns nothing (deprecated for all sites May 2026). The head-term SERPs are held by domain authority we can't match this year; the winnable ground is the mid-tail, where competitor pages are demonstrably stale, wrong, or paywalled — Instrumentl's Moses Taylor page says "gave $0 in grants in 2011" under a mismatched EIN, and GrantStation gates the very fields (deadlines, application procedure, contact) that we serve free. Strategy: become the freshest, most correct, fully ungated page for every foundation we enrich — fix the metadata layer now, deepen toward the data benchmark next, then widen the taxonomy web before scaling past 3,400 pages.

§1 — WHERE WE STANDWhat we serve today

Live fetch of /foundations/ny/elton-john-aids-foundation-inc/ and the code behind it (apps/research/services/seo_metadata.py, templates/research/detail.html):

  • Solid: full SSR (no JS wall), canonical slug URLs with 301s from legacy forms, Organization JSON-LD with EIN, enrichment-gated noindex on ~133k thin shells, snapshot-backed sitemaps, IndexNow submitter, public edge caching with Last-Modified, AI-answer-engine-friendly robots.txt.
  • Title: Elton John AIDS Foundation Inc Grants — Program Focus. Qualifier words ("Program Focus", "Application Requirements") — no dollars, no location, no year.
  • Meta description: prose clauses, truncated at a word boundary with no cleanup — Moses Taylor's ends "…funds Health Care Access and". Dangling conjunction, live in the SERP.
  • og:description: "68 grants on file. " — thin; twitter:title/description are generic site boilerplate on every page.
  • Canonical inherits request.build_absolute_uri from base.html — paginated URLs (?grants_page=2) self-canonicalize with the query string instead of pointing to page 1.
  • ~1,368 words; no visible data-freshness stamp; FAQPage JSON-LD (see §3).
Crawl-health flag: first fetch of the Moses Taylor page returned a 502 (retry: 200). Transient 5xxs on a young domain teach Googlebot to slow down; worth watching the edge/origin error rate in GSC crawl stats.

§3 — POLICYThree Google rulings that shape the plan

FAQPage is a dead end. Rich results were restricted to gov/health sites in Aug 2023 and deprecated for all sites in May 2026 (Search Console reporting removed June 2026). Our FAQPage block earns nothing. ProfilePage is explicitly invalid for third-party org directories — don't adopt it.
  • Scaled-content abuse (Mar 2024, enforced through the Mar 2026 core update): scale, automation, and AI generation are each permissible — the violation test is pages that add "little value beyond restated feed data." Our enrichment-gated noindex is exactly the right posture; it must hold as the corpus grows toward 100k.
  • MonetaryGrant/FundingAgency (Instrumentl's play) has no rich-result type; its value is entity disambiguation. Cheap to add, don't expect a SERP feature from it.
  • BreadcrumbList remains a live rich-result type and we don't emit it. GrantMakers.io does.
Red-teamed 2026-07-23 — this section is superseded in part. Three adversarial reviews (SEO strategy, engineering reality, opportunity cost) upheld the diagnosis but corrected the plan; the governing version now lives in AG epic #1254. Key corrections: (1) a Phase −1 reality gate — GSC indexation/impression baseline, demand check, and 502 ops spike before any template change (changing 3,439 pages first destroys the control group); (2) titles keep the application-intent words and add one number, canary-rolled to ~10% of pages — the original number-first pattern re-aimed us at the 990 query class where ProPublica is strongest; (3) the 2,500–4,000-word target is deleted as cargo cult — the spec is "render all data we already hold, zero authoring" (templated-table bloat recreates the doorway-page risk §3 warns about); (4) Phase 0 gains a recrawl mechanism (one-shot IndexNow submit-all / lastmod bump — a template deploy otherwise sends Google no change signal); (5) Phase 1 items are mostly deltas — Filing History, board, avg-grant, and a ProPublica 990 link already render today; the new work is median/range stats, officer-comp render, SSR-ing more grant rows, and UX-only facets (server-side facet URLs are a crawl trap); (6) KPI is organic-attributed signups, not positions; freshness stamps bind only to pipeline timestamps; the GSC benchmark is automated, never a manual cadence. FAQPage markup goes but the visible Q&A text stays (it feeds the AI-answer channel). Off-page/link outreach stays deferred with the marketing push per the 2026-07-05 stance.

§4 — THE PLANFour phases, ordered by leverage per unit effort (original — see red-team box above and AG#1254 for the operative version)

0Metadata layer — template-level, all 3,439 pages at onceDays · 1–2 PRs
  1. Number-forward titles. seo_metadata.py → "{Name} Grants | {City}, {ST} | ${total_giving} Given ({fy_year})" with graceful fallbacks when financials are absent. Every ranking competitor leads with place + dollars + year.
  2. Rebuild meta descriptions fact-first — "provided $X in grants in FY20XX; assets $Y; funds A, B, C; accepts unsolicited applications" — and fix the truncator to drop trailing conjunctions/stopwords. Kills the dangling-"and" SERP snippet.
  3. Canonical override on detail pages: emit get_absolute_url(), not request.build_absolute_uri; paginated params canonicalize to page 1.
  4. Fix the social card: og:description = the real meta description; twitter:title/description page-specific, not site boilerplate.
  5. Swap structured data: drop FAQPage; keep Organization (EIN, sameAs → own site + ProPublica); add BreadcrumbList; add WebPage with dateModified from the enrichment timestamp we already compute for the Last-Modified header.
  6. Visible freshness stamp: "IRS 990-PF data through FY20XX · enrichment updated <date>" in a <time> element near the top. Instrumentl and ProPublica both surface this; we already have the timestamps.
1Content depth — toward the 2,500–4,000-word data benchmarkWeeks
  1. Serve ungated what they blur: average / median / range grant size, full grantee list with year + state facets, contact block. Instrumentl blurs these behind 12 CTAs; GrantStation paywalls them. This is the differentiator — say so on-page.
  2. Multi-year financial table (ProPublica-style: assets, giving, revenue by fiscal year) expanding the existing Filing History section; link out to 990 PDFs (ProPublica hosts them — outbound links to authority cost nothing).
  3. Key people / board — already rendered; make sure names are crawlable text and add officer-compensation years where the 990 data has them.
  4. Synthesized application guidance stays the value-add. Under the scaled-content policy, this enrichment prose is precisely what makes our pages "more than restated feed data" — keep it above the tables, not instead of them.
2Taxonomy web — the Cause IQ lessonWeeks, parallel to 1
  1. Deepen the hubs we already have (/foundations/<state>/, category pages): every indexable profile reachable from ≥2 hub paths with real crawlable pagination, counts, and intro copy; hubs link profiles, profiles link back (visible breadcrumbs matching the BreadcrumbList markup).
  2. Add focus-area list pages (from enrichment program-area data — "Foundations funding health care access in PA") once hub plumbing is proven. These are the pages that can rank for non-brand queries no profile page can.
  3. Metro-level hubs only if state+category shows traction — Cause IQ runs them, but they're multiplier, not foundation.
3Scale governance & measurementOngoing
  1. Hold the noindex quality bar while growing 3,400 → 100k. Stage the rollout; watch GSC "Discovered – currently not indexed" as the crawl-budget canary on a young domain.
  2. Chase the 502s — transient 5xx at the edge/origin throttles crawl on exactly the domain-age profile that can least afford it.
  3. Benchmark a fixed query basket (the 7 research foundations × "grants" / "grant application" / "990") in GSC monthly; we never systematically enumerated today's SERPs — capture position-zero state before Phase 0 ships so the delta is measurable.

What we deliberately are not doing

  • No FAQPage or ProfilePage markup (deprecated / invalid).
  • No bulk-indexing the 133k thin shells to chase scale — that's the scaled-content-abuse profile on a young domain.
  • No gating. Ungated correctness is the moat the incumbents structurally can't copy without breaking their business model.

§5 — HONEST LIMITSCaveats from verification

  • Domain authority is the unmeasured co-factor. ProPublica ranks with a boilerplate meta description; on-page parity will not beat DR 90 for head terms. The plan's payoff window is mid-tail and long-tail first.
  • Cause IQ's per-foundation profile pages were never verified (Cloudflare bot wall); its evidence is a Wayback capture of one taxonomy hub.
  • No full SERP enumeration survived verification — only one live 990-query check. Phase 3's query-basket benchmark closes this hole.
  • One claim was refuted and excluded: our pages are not deeper than Instrumentl's grant-program pages — the openness advantage is real, the depth advantage is not (yet).

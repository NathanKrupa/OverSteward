SUMMARY:
For "<foundation name> grants/990" queries, the top-serving competitor pages fall into two archetypes: high-authority data-depth plays (ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer: millions of EIN-keyed pages of pure IRS financial tables with explicit freshness stamps; Cause IQ: ~192k foundation profiles fanned out from ~300 NTEE-category, state, metro, and grant-list taxonomy hubs) and gated lead-capture plays (Instrumentl: fact-forward metadata, 2,500-4,200-word section sets, MonetaryGrant JSON-LD, predicted current-year financials, with high-value fields blurred behind signup; GrantStation: member-paywalled profiles with only a ~5-grant preview). On policy, Google's scaled-content-abuse rules (March 2024, still enforced through 2026) permit large programmatic directories only when each page adds genuine value beyond scraped 990 feeds; aigranthelper's FAQPage JSON-LD earns nothing (FAQ rich results were restricted to gov/health in Aug 2023 and fully deprecated for all sites in May 2026) and ProfilePage markup is explicitly invalid for third-party org directories.
